Hardware Mode

Hardware Mode requires a physical Arduino-based keypad to unlock your vault. This adds a powerful layer of two-factor authentication that is fully disconnected from the internet.

If you enable this, you must enter a 6-digit PIN on your physical keypad before your Master Password will be accepted.
